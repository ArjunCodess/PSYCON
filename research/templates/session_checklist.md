# Session checklist

- [ ] Approval ID and protocol version match the approved study.
- [ ] Participant eligibility and current consent are confirmed.
- [ ] Audio, transcription, and voice-profile choices are recorded separately.
- [ ] Withdrawal status is clear and the participant knows how to stop.
- [ ] Hardware, battery, insulation, GSR safety, calibration, and firmware checks pass.
- [ ] No charger or wired external power is connected during wear.
- [ ] Wrist and audio placement match the protocol.
- [ ] One session ID and synchronized backend epoch are active.
- [ ] Status, battery, environment, motion, and errors are recorded.
- [ ] Both modules stop safely and the stop reason is recorded.
- [ ] Raw objects are hashed, backed up, and listed in the immutable manifest.
- [ ] Quality and inclusion decisions use controlled reason codes.
- [ ] Free-text fields contain no identifying information.
