# Group PSYCON runtime

The worker uses Community-1 revision `3533c8cf8e369892e6b79ff1bf80f7b0286a54ee` on shared 16 kHz PCM. Set `HF_TOKEN` after accepting the model's access conditions. The Compose worker passes that token and uses CUDA; the image installs pyannote.audio, SpeechBrain, faster-whisper, Praat-Parselmouth, and CUDA torchaudio.

The CUDA worker includes the [TalkNet-ASD](https://github.com/TaoRuijie/TalkNet-ASD) MIT implementation pinned at `6d6821479af485e251c4991487e40573b42181b4`. Its TalkSet weights are fetched at image build from revision `a67cf01a10acdf5c3b56dcee865cab922d8e6b77` with SHA-256 `d985ddd07dca08864a28337726f5b7d91b6426bebc7327f0e9b21cf9ff1cc937`. YuNet detection and SFace recognition come from the [OpenCV model zoo](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo), pinned at `d4938dfc9d4ec5d098bfa33e98b3f3345a236586`. Their SHA-256 digests are `8f2383e4dd3cfbb4553ea8718107fc0423210dc964f9f4280604804ed2552fa4` and `0ba9fbfa01b5270c96627c4ef784da859931e02f04419c829e83484087c34e79`. The marked frame's orientation and face identity vectors are carried into later analysis and reviewer playback.

TalkNet scores up to 32 clean windows per acoustic cluster, combining early turns with samples distributed across the recording. SFace reacquires the marked person at each window and verifies identity at multiple frames. A visual turn passes either a strong absolute active-speaker score, or a lower score with a large separation from competing visible faces. The latter still needs repeated turns whose voice embeddings agree at 0.65 or above. A borderline first window in a long clean turn triggers a disjoint second window; both must favor the same verified face. Two directly visible turns in separate acoustic clusters can support a profile when one is especially clear, the other passes this two-window check, their embeddings clear the 0.55 voice gate, and neither voice is closer to another face's established cohort. This avoids spreading a mistaken Community-1 cluster label. Other propagated clean turns must clear the 0.55 voice gate and favor their face's voice cohort over competing cohorts. Uncertain and contradictory turns remain available for reviewer correction.

Overlap is excluded from feature extraction. For playback, an overlapping Community-1 span can be included for a face through a repeated cluster link or a nearby clean attributed turn, and the supporting source segment is recorded. This is the original mixed PCM, so overlapping speakers can be audible in more than one participant's export. Review confirmation can promote a clean turn into training eligibility; a single isolated automated attribution cannot. Training requires at least three usable clean seconds and the current PSYCON feature schema.
