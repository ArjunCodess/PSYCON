# Consent document review

On 12 October 2026, nine photographs supplied in the private `consents/` directory were visually reviewed. Each shows a PSYCON group-session consent sheet with participant entries. These are nine photographed sheets, not a verified count of unique people or covered recordings. Repeated names occur across sheets, and some fields are incomplete.

The printed statement covers agreement to take part in one group discussion, voluntary participation, the right to stop, and the absence of medical or psychological diagnosis. Several participant entries carry handwritten September 2026 dates. These dates are recorded statements on the forms; the photograph filenames do not establish when consent was obtained or whether it preceded collection.

The visible statement does not explicitly cover audio/video recording, automated behavioral analysis, model training, data retention, access, publication, or withdrawal of stored data. The sheets do not contain a parent/guardian permission section or an ethics-board approval decision. Participant ages and signature authenticity have not been independently verified. Some session identifiers, topics and dates are blank, so linking every sheet to the retained recordings remains pending.

After the photographic review, the authors confirmed that the complete records document recording and model-training permissions, applicable parental permission and prior approval, including details outside the supplied photographs. This confirmation is recorded as author-provided information and has not been independently checked. The photographs establish signed participation records; they do not independently certify the additional permissions or approval. No approving-body name, approval identifier or approval date has been invented. The limits of the photographic review are not a finding that the confirmed documentation is absent.

Society for Science distinguishes prior IRB review, participant consent/assent and written parental permission for minors in its [human-participant rules](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/international-rules/human-participants/). The relevant approval decision and protocol must establish the permitted scope; these photographs alone cannot do that.

The signed photographs remain local and are excluded from Git. Public documentation omits names, signatures, class/section identifiers and participant-specific dates. No database permission flag, model artifact, eligibility decision or performance result was changed by this document review.
