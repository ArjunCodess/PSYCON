\GuideTitle{Items A--J}
\textbf{How answers work.} A score of 0 does not support the named pattern. A score of 1 records one weak moment. A score of 2 marks a possible one-session pattern that needs more evidence. Scores of 3 or 4 allow the psychologist or model to report the pattern for this session, with timestamps and context. N/O means no conclusion.

\vspace{1.5mm}
{\scriptsize\textbf{Interpretation rule:} These names describe patterns inside one recorded discussion. They do not establish why the pattern occurred, whether it occurs elsewhere, or whether the participant has a psychological condition. Scores Q--T require a same-participant baseline and an identifiable event; otherwise use N/O.}
