# Official engineering references

These links were checked on 8 September 2026. Verify the exact purchased module and board revision before applying any pin, voltage, current, thermal, charging, or layout value. Breakout boards may add regulators, pull-ups, dividers, protection parts, or different pin labels.

- [ESP32 Series Datasheet, Espressif](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32_datasheet_en.pdf)
- [MAX30102 Datasheet, Analog Devices](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/MAX30102.pdf)
- [ADS1115 product page and current datasheet, Texas Instruments](https://www.ti.com/product/ADS1115)
- [MCP9808 product page and DS20005095, Microchip](https://www.microchip.com/en-us/product/MCP9808)
- [MPU-6000 and MPU-6050 Product Specification, TDK InvenSense](https://invensense.tdk.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/MPU-6000-Datasheet.pdf)
- [INMP441 Datasheet, TDK InvenSense](https://invensense.tdk.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/INMP441.pdf)
- [TEMT6000X01 Datasheet, Vishay](https://www.vishay.com/docs/81579/temt6000.pdf)
- [TP4056 product page, Nanjing Top Power ASIC](https://www.toppwr.com/eproduct/view.php?id=478)

The purchased TP4056 board must be identified separately. A TP4056 charger IC does not by itself prove that a module includes cell overcharge, over-discharge, over-current, reverse-polarity, load sharing, or safe charge-while-operating behavior.
