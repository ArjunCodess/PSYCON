## What participation involves

You will wear a wrist device that records pulse-related optical signals, skin conductance, skin temperature, and movement. A separate device may record ambient light and, only if you agree below, speech. The session lasts about [duration] and includes [approved tasks]. You may pause or stop at any time without giving a reason or losing any benefit to which you are entitled.

This prototype studies non-clinical patterns in sensor data. It does not diagnose a health condition, provide medical advice, or replace care from a qualified professional.

## Speech and voice processing

If you consent to audio, the device records [continuous or scheduled] speech during [approved parts]. The project processes recordings locally to calculate acoustic features and, if approved, transcripts and language features. Recordings may contain other people's voices, so collection must follow the approved bystander procedure: [procedure].

Audio consent does not include creation of a biometric wearer voice profile. If you separately consent to a voice profile, three short recordings produce an encrypted average voice embedding. The source enrollment recordings are deleted after processing. The profile is used only to distinguish the enrolled wearer from anonymous recording-local speaker labels.

## Risks and choices

Possible risks include temporary discomfort or skin irritation, unintended capture of speech, privacy loss if data are disclosed, and inaccurate research outputs. Tell the operator immediately if the device feels hot, painful, or uncomfortable. The operator will stop the session if the equipment becomes unsafe or if required consent no longer applies.
