Processing permission

The first transcription downloads the local Whisper model. It may take several minutes; the recording is not sent to a transcription API.

02 / METHOD

What happens next

  1. 01

    The WAV is decoded, mixed to mono, and resampled to 16 kHz PCM16.

  2. 02

    Each window of at most two seconds is wrapped in a checksum-protected Protocol v2 packet.

  3. 03

    psycon_audio measures level, clipping, pitch statistics, voice stability, timing, and spectrum.

  4. 04

    Every window receives an explicit quality decision before future inference.

RESEARCH BOUNDARY

This checks recording quality, not mental health.

A usable result means the signal passed today’s engineering thresholds. It does not identify a speaker, interpret words, diagnose a condition, or prove that the final wearable microphone is calibrated.
